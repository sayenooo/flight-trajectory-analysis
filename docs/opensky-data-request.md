# OpenSky Data Access Scope

## Project

Aircraft trajectory analysis and clustering for the ZRH–GVA route.

## Purpose

Non-commercial university coursework / academic research.

## Requested area

Switzerland, with emphasis on the corridor between:
- Zurich Airport — ZRH / LSZH
- Geneva Airport — GVA / LSGG

The surrounding departure, en-route and arrival areas are included because complete trajectories may extend beyond a narrow line between the airports.

## Requested time period

Preferred:
- 2025-01-01 through 2025-12-31

Fallback:
- Any continuous 3–6 month period in 2025 if the full year is unavailable.

## Intended analysis

- trajectory reconstruction
- preprocessing and visualization
- comparison of repeated flights
- HDBSCAN clustering
- detection and interpretation of unusual trajectories

## Desired fields

Where available:
- timestamps
- ICAO24 identifiers
- callsigns
- latitude / longitude
- barometric and geometric altitude
- velocity
- heading / track
- vertical rate
- departure and arrival information
